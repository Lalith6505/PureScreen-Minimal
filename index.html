Privacy Policy for PureScreen Minimal

Effective Date: September 17, 2026
Developer: Lalith Madanayake (LP Residence, Kottawa, Western Province, Sri Lanka)

1. Overview & Core Philosophy

PureScreen Minimal is an offline-first, distraction-free minimalist Android launcher designed to reduce screen time and restore digital well-being. We believe your device usage, habits, and application choices are strictly your personal property. PureScreen Minimal does not collect, transmit, sell, or monetize any of your personal data.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Zero Data Collected: PureScreen Minimal does not collect any personal data, analytics, device identifiers, IP addresses, location data, or telemetry.

  • Installed Applications: PureScreen Minimal queries installed launcher activities strictly on your local device to render your application drawer and home screen shortcuts. This list is never transmitted anywhere.
  • User Customizations: Your pinned apps, hidden apps, custom app names, mindful delay settings, and theme preferences are stored strictly on your device using Android's encrypted DataStore.
  • Focus / Monk Mode: All focus session timers and schedules operate entirely locally on your device.

3. Network & Internet Permissions

PureScreen Minimal does not request or require the Android Internet permission (android.permission.INTERNET). The application has no technical capability to connect to the internet, cloud servers, or third-party tracking services.

4. Third-Party Services & SDKs

PureScreen Minimal contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero analytics trackers, and zero external telemetry frameworks. No data is shared with Google, Facebook, advertisers, or any data brokers.

5. Children's Privacy

PureScreen Minimal does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. It is safe for all age demographics.

6. Security

All application preferences are saved within your device's sandboxed private application storage, protected by Android operating system permissions.
